# Product

## Register

product

## Users

IT administrators and system configurators. They manage user accounts, permissions, platform settings, and integrations. Login is infrequent but purposeful — they're accessing a serious operations system on behalf of an organization, not a casual tool. They expect a fast, trustworthy entry point that signals capability and control.

## Product Purpose

Newtech Unified CXM: a contact center operations platform used by Newtech Software, Newtech Global, and Newtech Teleservices. Manages AI agents, outbound campaigns, and customer experience workflows at scale. The login gate is the first thing system administrators see — it should reflect the seriousness and brand identity of the platform they're about to configure.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, identity-forward. The platform is a control system, not a consumer app. Three words: **confident, branded, capable**.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS login (Salesforce, HubSpot): stock photo splits, forgettable, template-feeling
- Dark hacker aesthetic (Vercel, Linear dark): all-black neon — wrong brand register, too developer-tool
- Consumer-soft (Notion, Loom): rounded, pastel, playful — too casual for enterprise admin access
- Overcrowded marketing page: testimonials and feature bullets on login — distracting, inappropriate context

## Design Principles

1. **Brand before form** — the login is a brand moment, not just a utility gate. Identity is present and deliberate.
2. **Confident restraint** — strong brand presence achieved through composition and color strategy, not visual noise.
3. **Zero ambiguity** — admin users login infrequently; the path from landing to authenticated must be clear and fast.
4. **System signals trust** — the visual design should communicate that this is enterprise-grade infrastructure, not a side project.
5. **One accent, one action** — Newtech Green owns the CTA. No competing colors pulling attention away from the login button.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Focus ring visible on all interactive elements (green focus ring per design system). Keyboard navigable in logical order. Color never the sole differentiator for error states.
